Humanity didn't merely lose technology. It lost **history**.

The survivors couldn't communicate across continents. Languages diverged. Cultures evolved independently. Thousands of years passed.

The actual Churning became mythology. And eventually: **mythology became religion.**

The people of your starting sanctuary believe the Churning happened — but they don't know who performed it, why it happened, what Amṛta or Hālāhala actually are, whether the Firstborn existed, or how many Kuns still remain.

And most importantly: they don't know **the Churning never actually stopped.**
