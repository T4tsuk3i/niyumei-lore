Before Earth had oceans, mountains or people, there was only what the oldest surviving fragments call:

**Ādi-Sāgara.**

*The First Ocean.*

Not a physical ocean. It was the primordial medium from which reality condensed.

Some cultures called it **the Deep**. Others: **the Dark Before Dawn**, **the Endless**, **Te Kore** — the void, **Nammu**, **Akasha**.

The names were different. They were all describing the same thing.

> A reality beneath reality.
