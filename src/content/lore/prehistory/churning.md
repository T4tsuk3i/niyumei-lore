description: The Great Churning — when the mechanism failed

---

The Firstborn constructed an impossible machine. Or perhaps it wasn't a machine. Perhaps it was a mountain. Perhaps it was both.

In your universe, the truth is stranger:

- **Mandara** wasn't merely a mountain. It was an **anchor** — a structure that reached through dimensions.
- **Vāsuki** wasn't merely a serpent. It was a living cosmic conduit.

And the Firstborn began **the Mahā-Manthana** — the Great Churning.

They pulled reality itself back and forth. And the universe responded.

Among the first things to emerge was **Amṛta**. Then something nobody expected: **Hālāhala** — the accumulated entropy of reality, everything reality had rejected.

When the mechanism failed — Mandara shifted, Vāsuki screamed, the First Ocean rose — the ancient world experienced **the Mahā-Pralaya**. The Great Dissolution.

> The Churning did not create the new world. It opened the door to it. And something on the other side noticed.
