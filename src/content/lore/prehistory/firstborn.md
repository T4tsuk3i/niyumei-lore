The oldest civilizations told different stories about the beings that came before humanity.

Some called them **Devas**. Others **Anunnaki**, **Atua**, **Titans**, **Jotnar**. Some simply called them **the Firstborn**.

Your game never confirms whether these were actually different species. Maybe they were. Maybe they were different cultures describing the same beings. Maybe they weren't biological creatures at all.

## Siddhi

They possessed something humanity would later call **Siddhi** — extraordinary capability. Not magic in the modern fantasy sense. They could interact directly with the fundamental forces of reality: alter matter, shape life, move energy, enter dreams, change weather.

And, most importantly: **they could interact with Ādi-Sāgara.**
