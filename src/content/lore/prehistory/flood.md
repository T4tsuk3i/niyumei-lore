The oceans rose. Not because Earth received more water — because the boundary between the physical ocean and Ādi-Sāgara collapsed.

The sea became infinite. It swallowed continents, civilizations, temples, cities, forests, mountains, entire cultures. The sky itself appeared to drown.

There wasn't one ark. There were thousands.

Most failed. Some became buried civilizations. Some became the foundations of modern settlements. Some are still drifting beneath the oceans.

And one day, the player will discover one.
