Vallum are those who learned to withstand the altered world. Guards. Defenders. Wardens.

> "Nothing reaches those who stand behind us."

## Their expression

Where Vox move *through*, Vallum *hold*. Their affinity is less about light and more about weight — stone, anchor, oath. They believe the altered world can be endured, not explained.

## Creed

- Stand where the breach is found.
- Let the wall be the last thing Hālāhala touches.
- Never trade the horizon for a gift.
