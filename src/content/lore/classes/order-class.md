The Order's class are those who learned to **restore** the altered world. Healers. Scholars. Ritualists. Keepers of knowledge.

> "What was broken can be made whole."

## Their expression

They channel Amṛta not as power but as *mending* — wounds closing, wells brightening, fear quieted. Every Order ritual is built around one assumption the other classes never make:

> The world is not hostile. It is wounded.

Whether they are right is the central argument of the Order.
