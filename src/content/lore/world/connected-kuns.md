Eventually the player discovers the truth that rearranges everything.

The Kuns aren't random. They're **connected** — across Earth, like nodes. And they're all connected to something beneath the planet.

Something enormous. Something ancient. Something that was **already there before the Churning.**

And the Churning wasn't actually an attempt to create Amṛta.

> It was an attempt to **wake it up**.

The Firstborn weren't trying to obtain immortality. They were trying to contact whatever existed beneath reality.

And they succeeded.
