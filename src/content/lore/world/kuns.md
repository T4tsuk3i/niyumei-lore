The flood eventually receded, but small pockets of Amṛta had fallen into the world. Wherever they accumulated, life could survive.

Water became the vessel. And so the first **Kun** was born — a small body of water infused with Amṛta.

Around a Kun:

- plants grew
- animals survived
- disease diminished
- crops flourished
- wounds healed
- people could live

The first survivors gathered around these places. Kuns aren't merely magical ponds. **They are scars left by the Churning.**

Thousands of them still exist. Like nodes. All connected to something beneath the planet.
