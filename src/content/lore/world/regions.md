The flood receded, but the world was no longer Earth as humanity remembered. The continents had changed. The climate had changed. The oceans had changed.

Different regions developed different manifestations:

- **The Weeping Forests** — where dead things returned incorrectly
- **The Ashen Expanse** — where Hālāhala barrenized everything it touched
- **The Drowned Cities** — buried temples still drifting beneath the water
- **The Impossible Lands** — places where reality itself behaves differently
- **The Unknown Territories** — lands that hum back

Humanity scattered. And every surviving civilization developed its own explanation. One said the gods fought. Another, the ancestors failed. Another, the world was punished. Another, the sky broke. Another, the sea ate the earth. Another, *we were never supposed to survive.*

**All of them were partially correct.**
