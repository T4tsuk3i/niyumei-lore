The survivors built **Vāra**. Not one. Hundreds.

Massive underground sanctuaries designed around Kuns.

Some survived. Some failed. Some were sealed. Some became cities. Some became tombs. And the people inside eventually forgot there had ever been an outside world.

Your sanctuary is one of them.
