One of the Firstborn tried to contain Hālāhala.

That being became the basis of humanity's later stories of **Śiva** — and **Nīlakaṇṭha**, *the blue-throated one*.

In your universe, the event has a different consequence. The being succeeded.

**Partially.**

Hālāhala was contained. Not destroyed. A fraction entered the world.

## Why the throat

The oldest account says the Guardian drank the poison to keep it from spreading — and that the color left in its throat never faded. Later religions made a metaphor of a fact:

> The world was saved by a wound that never closed.
