The Guardians interpret the Order's oldest warnings *as metaphor* — and act on them anyway.

Their creed is older than the sanctuary itself:

> Protect the Kun. Protect the people. The rest is story.

## What they actually believe

- the Churning was an accident of history
- the Hālāhala is contained forever
- the Order's unease is superstition dressed in old paper

They are not wrong to protect the sanctuary. They are wrong about what it is.
