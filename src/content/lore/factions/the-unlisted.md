Not every survivor gathered around a Kun.

Some fled the other way — into the deep oceans, into the ashen inland, into the dark between the stars of the world.

No one in the sanctuary knows what they became. The oldest maps describe them only as:

> **???** — the territories that hum back.

They are mentioned in no faction's doctrine. But Vāra-keeper records hint that someone, out there, still remembers *the other side* of the Churning.
