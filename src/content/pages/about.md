**Niyumei** is an original dark-fantasy setting: a world that did not flood so much as sink back into the ocean it was made from. This archive is its canon, recorded as the survivors might have kept it. The record is partial, contradictory in places, and written long after the fact.

## How the archive is arranged

Everything is filed under one of five **Ages**, from the First Ocean to the Present. Within an age, entries run in the order you'd want to read them. The *Previous* and *Next* links at the bottom of each entry walk the whole record from beginning to end.

Entries are also sorted into **sections**: prehistory, the world, mythology, factions, and classes. Use the filters at the top of the [Archive](/lore) to narrow things down.

The [Timeline](/timeline) is the short version: one beat per era, each linked to the entry that tells it in full.

## Finding things

Press <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> anywhere to search. Search ignores diacritics, so typing *amrta* finds **Amṛta**.

## A note on canon

The archive grows as the world does. Entries may be revised, and the in-world sources quoted here are not always reliable narrators. That is part of the point.
